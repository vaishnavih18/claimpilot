# Home Insurance Policy Wording

## Declarations

- Sum Insured: $450,000
- Policy Basis: sum_insured
- Excess: $750

## 1. Definitions

### HOME-1.1 Insured Property
The insured property means the residential dwelling located at the address shown in the policy schedule, including all permanently fixed structures forming part of the building envelope, internal fixtures and fittings that are affixed to the structure, and any detached garage, shed or outbuilding on the same premises. The insured property does not include land, landscaping, or structures used for business purposes.

### HOME-1.2 Sum Insured
The sum insured of $450,000 represents the maximum amount we will pay for any one claim, and is based on the full replacement cost of the insured property as declared by you. This amount applies on a sum insured basis, meaning we will not pay more than this limit regardless of the actual cost of reinstatement or repair.

### HOME-1.3 Excess
The excess of $750 is the amount you must contribute towards each and every claim. This excess is the first amount deducted from any claim payment we make, and you remain responsible for this amount even if the claim is covered under this policy.

### HOME-1.4 Claim
A claim means a demand made by you for payment under this policy following loss of or damage to the insured property that you believe is covered by the policy wording. A claim must be notified to us in writing within 30 days of the loss or damage occurring, or as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter.

### HOME-1.5 Replacement Cost
Replacement cost means the cost of replacing, repairing or reinstating the insured property to the same condition, quality and extent as it was immediately before the loss or damage occurred, using materials of similar kind and quality. Replacement cost is assessed at current market rates and does not include any allowance for depreciation or betterment.

## 2. Significant Features and Benefits

### HOME-2.1 Sum Insured Basis of Cover

This policy operates on a sum insured basis, meaning the maximum amount we will pay for any claim is limited to the sum insured of $450,000 shown in your policy schedule. You are responsible for ensuring the sum insured accurately reflects the full replacement cost of your home and contents as declared. If the sum insured is insufficient to cover the full cost of reinstatement following a claim, we will only pay up to the sum insured, less any applicable excess.

### HOME-2.2 Standard Excess Application

A standard excess of $750 applies to each claim you make under this policy. This excess represents the amount you must contribute towards the cost of any claim, and we will only pay the remaining balance up to the sum insured. The excess must be paid before we release settlement funds for your claim.

### HOME-2.3 Automatic Inflation Adjustment

To help protect your cover from the effects of inflation, we will automatically increase your sum insured each year on your policy renewal date by the percentage movement in the Consumer Price Index (All Groups, Australia). This adjustment will be reflected in your renewal notice, and you may request an additional increase at any time if you believe your sum insured is inadequate.

### HOME-2.4 Reinstatement of Sum Insured After Claim

Following a claim that we have paid in full, your sum insured will be automatically reinstated to its original amount at no additional premium, provided you pay the applicable excess. This reinstatement applies only once per claim period and does not apply if you have failed to pay any premium or breach any condition of this policy.

## 3. Buildings Cover

### HOME-3.1 Scope of Cover

We will pay for loss or damage to the buildings and structures that form part of your residential dwelling, including fixed fixtures and fittings, up to the sum insured of $450,000. This cover applies to the main residence and any detached structures such as garages, sheds, and carports located on the same premises. Cover is provided on an agreed value basis, meaning we have accepted the sum insured as the full replacement value of the buildings at the time of loss.

### HOME-3.2 Insurable Building Components

Buildings cover includes the structural elements of your home such as walls, roof, floors, doors, windows, and permanent built-in fixtures including kitchen cabinets, bathroom fittings, and fixed flooring materials. We also cover permanently installed services such as electrical wiring, plumbing, gas lines, and heating or cooling systems that are integral to the building. Permanent alterations and extensions that have been added to the dwelling are included, provided they do not increase the replacement cost beyond the sum insured.

### HOME-3.3 Exclusions from Buildings Cover

We do not cover loss or damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or lack of maintenance, nor do we cover damage resulting from faulty design, faulty materials, or faulty workmanship unless caused by an insured peril. We exclude damage caused by subsidence, landslip, or ground heave unless resulting from an insured event. We also do not cover loss or damage to structures used for business purposes or structures that are not permanently affixed to the land.

### HOME-3.4 Excess and Claim Settlement

For each claim under Buildings Cover, you must pay the excess of $750 towards the cost of repairs or replacement. We will settle claims on the basis of repair or replacement at current market rates, provided the cost does not exceed the sum insured. If the cost of repair exceeds the sum insured, we will pay up to the sum insured less the excess.

### HOME-3.5 Reinstatement of Sum Insured

If you make a claim under Buildings Cover, the sum insured will be reinstated automatically for the remainder of the period of insurance at no additional premium, provided you notify us of the claim within the required timeframe. If the cost of reinstatement after a loss exceeds the sum insured by more than 10%, you must increase the sum insured to reflect the current replacement cost to maintain full cover.

### HOME-3.6 Underinsurance Provision

If at the time of loss the sum insured is less than the full replacement cost of the buildings, we will apply an underinsurance penalty and settle your claim on a proportionate basis. For example, if the sum insured represents 80% of the replacement cost, we will pay 80% of the claim amount less the excess. You are responsible for ensuring the sum insured is adequate and reflects the current replacement value of your dwelling.

## 4. Contents Cover

### HOME-4.1 What We Cover

We will cover your personal contents and household goods that are your property and located within the insured dwelling, up to the sum insured of $450,000. Contents cover applies to items you own or are responsible for, including furniture, appliances, clothing, books, and other domestic items. We will pay the cost of replacement or repair following an insured event, less any applicable excess of $750. This cover is provided on a sum insured basis, meaning the total amount we will pay for all claims in any one period of insurance cannot exceed $450,000.

### HOME-4.2 Exclusions from Contents Cover

We do not cover money, coins, banknotes, securities, or documents of any kind. We exclude motor vehicles, trailers, caravans, and parts thereof, as well as items in outbuildings unless specifically endorsed. We do not cover items used for business purposes or professional trade, nor do we cover damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or inherent defect. Damage resulting from faulty workmanship, lack of maintenance, or failure to take reasonable precautions is also excluded.

### HOME-4.3 Special Items and Limits

Certain items including jewellery, watches, furs, artworks, and collectibles are limited to $2,500 per item unless specifically listed and endorsed on your policy schedule. Portable electronic devices such as laptops, tablets, and mobile phones are covered only for loss or damage occurring within the insured dwelling. Bicycles are limited to $500 per item unless separately endorsed. Any item exceeding the special limit must be declared separately to obtain adequate cover.

### HOME-4.4 Claims Procedure and Verification

You must notify us of any loss or damage as soon as practicable and in any case within 30 days of discovery. You must provide reasonable proof of ownership and value, including receipts, photographs, or bank statements where available. We may require you to obtain independent valuations or quotes for repair or replacement. Failure to provide adequate documentation may result in a reduction of the claim amount or rejection of the claim.

### HOME-4.5 Basis of Settlement

We will settle claims on a replacement as new basis for items less than ten years old, provided the item is not obsolete or no longer manufactured. For items older than ten years, we will apply a deduction for age and condition to reflect the pre-loss value. The total claim payment, including all items claimed, will not exceed your sum insured of $450,000 less the excess of $750. Where multiple items are claimed, we will apply the excess once per claim event.

## 5. General Exclusions

### HOME-5.1 Wear and Tear
We do not cover loss or damage caused by or arising from wear and tear, gradual deterioration, weathering, rust, corrosion, or any process of gradual decline in the condition of the insured property. This exclusion applies regardless of whether such deterioration was accelerated by any other cause.

### HOME-5.2 Intentional Damage
We do not cover loss or damage that you, or any person acting with your knowledge or consent, have intentionally caused to the insured property. This exclusion applies even if the damage was caused to mitigate a greater loss.

### HOME-5.3 Unlawful Activities
We do not cover loss or damage arising from or in connection with any criminal act, unlawful activity, or breach of law committed by you or any occupant of the insured property. This exclusion does not apply to loss or damage caused by theft or burglary by third parties.

### HOME-5.4 Flood
We do not cover loss or damage caused by or arising from flood, being the inundation of normally dry land by the escape of water from the normal confines of any lake, river, stream, watercourse, canal, or artificial water storage facility. This exclusion applies regardless of the cause of the flood.

### HOME-5.5 Maintenance and Repair Obligations
We do not cover loss or damage that could have been prevented or reduced by the performance of routine maintenance, repairs, or inspections. You are responsible for maintaining the insured property in a state of good repair and condition.

### HOME-5.6 Consequential Loss
We do not cover any consequential loss, indirect loss, loss of use, loss of enjoyment, loss of profit, or any loss that does not directly result from the insured peril. This exclusion applies even if such consequential loss was reasonably foreseeable.

## 6. Specific Peril and Regional Exclusions

### HOME-6.1 Cyclone Damage – Queensland and Northern Territory

We do not cover loss or damage caused by cyclone, including storm surge, in Queensland or the Northern Territory, unless you have elected Cyclone Cover as an optional extra on your policy schedule. This exclusion applies to wind speeds exceeding 90 km/h associated with a classified cyclonic system.

### HOME-6.2 Severe Bushfire – Victoria and New South Wales

We do not cover loss or damage caused by bushfire or grassfire in Victoria or New South Wales occurring during declared fire danger periods, except where the insured property is located within an urban area with established perimeter fire mitigation infrastructure. This exclusion does not apply if you maintain current certification under the relevant state's bushfire protection standard.

### HOME-6.3 Hailstorm Damage – South Australia

We do not cover loss or damage to the roof, guttering, fascia, or external fixtures caused by hail in South Australia, unless the property is fitted with approved hail-resistant roofing materials certified to withstand hail impact of 40 mm diameter or greater. Claims for hail damage in Adelaide Hills and Mid-Murray regions require submission of pre-loss photographic evidence.

### HOME-6.4 Severe Dust Storm – Western Australia

We do not cover loss or damage caused by dust storms exceeding 50 km/h wind speeds in Western Australia, including damage to air conditioning units, solar panels, and external ventilation systems. This exclusion applies to the Pilbara, Goldfields-Esperance, and Outback regions as defined by the Bureau of Meteorology.

### HOME-6.5 Bushfire – Tasmania

We do not cover loss or damage caused by bushfire in Tasmania occurring during the fire season (1 October to 30 April) unless the property is located within 100 metres of an all-weather access road and has maintained defensible space of at least 10 metres around the building perimeter.

## 7. Conditions

### HOME-7.1 Duty of Disclosure

You must take reasonable care to provide accurate and complete information when applying for this insurance and when making any changes to your circumstances. Any misstatement, omission or misrepresentation of material facts may result in your claim being declined or your policy being cancelled. You should notify us immediately of any changes that may affect your cover, including renovations, changes of use, or extended periods of vacancy.

### HOME-7.2 Maintenance and Care

You must maintain the insured dwelling in a good state of repair and take all reasonable precautions to prevent loss or damage. This includes maintaining the roof, gutters, drainage systems, and structural elements in sound condition. Failure to maintain the property may result in claims being declined if the loss would not have occurred but for such neglect.

### HOME-7.3 Notification of Loss

You must notify us of any loss, damage, or incident that may give rise to a claim as soon as practicable and in any case within 30 days. You should provide us with full details of the event, including the date, time, location, and nature of the loss. Failure to notify us within the specified timeframe may prejudice our ability to investigate and may result in the claim being declined.

### HOME-7.4 Claims Cooperation and Investigation

You must provide us with all reasonable assistance and information required to investigate and assess any claim. This includes providing copies of receipts, invoices, photographs, police reports, and any other supporting documentation we request. You must not admit liability or settle any claim without our prior written consent.

### HOME-7.5 Excess Application

An excess of $750 applies to each claim, except where we have agreed in writing to waive or modify it. The excess must be paid by you before we settle any claim. Where multiple perils contribute to a single loss event, only one excess will apply.

### HOME-7.6 Cancellation and Non-Renewal

We may cancel this policy by providing you with 30 days' written notice if you fail to pay the premium, breach any material condition of this policy, or provide false information. You may cancel this policy at any time by providing written notice, and we will refund any unearned premium on a pro-rata basis. Upon cancellation, all cover ceases immediately unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 8. Claims Process

### HOME-8.1 Notification of Loss
You must notify us of any loss, damage or claim as soon as practicable and in any case no later than 30 days after the event occurs or you become aware of it. Notification may be made by telephone, email, online portal or in writing. Failure to notify us within this timeframe may result in your claim being declined or reduced if we are prejudiced by the delay.

### HOME-8.2 Provision of Documentation
Upon notification of a claim, you must provide us with all relevant documentation including proof of ownership, receipts, invoices, photographs of damage, repair quotes and any other evidence we reasonably require to assess your claim. You must cooperate fully with our investigations and provide information within 14 days of our request unless we agree otherwise in writing.

### HOME-8.3 Assessment and Valuation
We will assess your claim based on the policy basis of sum insured and the terms, conditions and exclusions of this policy. For claims involving damage, we may arrange for our assessor to inspect the property at no cost to you. The amount we pay will not exceed the sum insured of $450,000 less the applicable excess of $750.

### HOME-8.4 Excess Application
The excess of $750 is the amount you must contribute towards each claim. This excess applies per claim event, not per item damaged. The excess will be deducted from any settlement amount we pay to you unless the claim is declined entirely.

### HOME-8.5 Payment of Claims
Once we have assessed and approved your claim, we will advise you of the settlement amount and arrange payment within 10 business days. Payment will be made by direct transfer to your nominated bank account or by another method agreed between us in writing.

## 9. Complaints and Dispute Resolution

### HOME-9.1 Internal Complaints Process

If you are dissatisfied with our service or a decision we have made, you may lodge a complaint with us in writing, by telephone, or through our online portal. We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within one business day and provide you with a reference number. We will investigate your complaint thoroughly and provide you with a written response within 30 calendar days, or if this is not possible, we will advise you of the expected timeframe and keep you informed of progress.

### HOME-9.2 External Dispute Resolution

If you are not satisfied with our response to your complaint, or if we have not responded within 30 calendar days, you may refer the matter to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA). AFCA is a free and independent dispute resolution scheme that can be contacted at 1300 931 937, by email at info@afca.org.au, or through their website at www.afca.org.au. AFCA's determination is binding on us, subject to your right to pursue other remedies available under law.

### HOME-9.3 Time Limits and Your Rights

You must lodge a complaint with us within two years of becoming aware of the issue giving rise to your complaint, or within two years of when you should reasonably have become aware of it. Nothing in this complaints procedure affects your rights under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) or any other applicable legislation, and you may pursue legal remedies at any time within the applicable limitation period.
