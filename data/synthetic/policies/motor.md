# Motor Insurance Policy Wording

## Declarations

- Sum Insured: $25,000
- Policy Basis: sum_insured
- Excess: $400

## 1. Definitions

### MOTOR-1.1 Insured Vehicle
The motor vehicle described in the Schedule, including its factory-fitted components, accessories, and spare parts contained within or upon the vehicle at the time of loss or damage. The Insured Vehicle is identified by its registration number and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) as specified in the Schedule.

### MOTOR-1.2 Sum Insured
The maximum amount of $25,000 representing the agreed value of the Insured Vehicle for the purposes of this policy. This amount is stated in the Schedule and forms the basis upon which the premium has been calculated and claims will be assessed.

### MOTOR-1.3 Excess
The amount of $400 that you must contribute towards any claim, except where a specific different excess is stated in the Schedule or where the claim arises from an uninsured driver or hail damage event. The Excess represents your share of the loss and must be paid before We release any claim payment to you.

### MOTOR-1.4 Insured
The person or entity named as the Policyholder in the Schedule, and any person who is a permitted driver as defined in this policy while driving the Insured Vehicle with your consent.

### MOTOR-1.5 We, Us, Our
Australian Motor Insurance Pty Ltd, the insurer issuing this policy and responsible for the payment of claims and administration of coverage under these terms and conditions.

## 2. Significant Features and Benefits

### MOTOR-2.1 Sum Insured Basis of Cover

This policy is arranged on a sum insured basis of $25,000. We will pay claims up to this amount, less your excess of $400, provided the claim is otherwise covered under this policy. The sum insured represents the maximum we will pay for any one claim, regardless of the actual value of your vehicle at the time of loss.

### MOTOR-2.2 Excess Application

Your excess of $400 applies to each and every claim you make under this policy. This excess represents the amount you must contribute towards the cost of any repair or replacement following a covered loss. The excess will be deducted from any claim payment we make to you.

### MOTOR-2.3 Agreed Value Protection

We agree that your vehicle is worth up to the sum insured of $25,000 for the purposes of this policy. In the event of a total loss, we will settle your claim based on this agreed value, provided you have not misrepresented the vehicle's condition or use at the time of application.

### MOTOR-2.4 24-Hour Claims Assistance

We provide access to our national claims support service available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Our claims team will guide you through the claims process and arrange emergency roadside assistance if required. Early notification of a claim helps us to manage your matter efficiently and fairly.

## 3. Vehicle Damage Cover

### MOTOR-3.1 Insured Events
We will pay for accidental loss or damage to your vehicle caused by collision, impact, fire, theft, malicious act, or weather event, provided the vehicle is being used in accordance with the terms of this policy. We will not pay for loss or damage arising from wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or gradual deterioration. The maximum we will pay for any one claim is the sum insured of $25,000, less your applicable excess of $400.

### MOTOR-3.2 Excess Application
Your excess of $400 applies to each claim for vehicle damage. If your vehicle is damaged as a result of a single event, only one excess applies regardless of the number of separate damage locations. Where a claim arises from theft or malicious damage, the excess remains applicable unless you have elected optional coverage that provides excess waiver for these events.

### MOTOR-3.3 Basis of Settlement
We settle claims on the basis of the sum insured. This means we will pay the cost of repair to your vehicle, or if your vehicle is beyond economical repair, we will pay the market value of the vehicle immediately before the loss, whichever is the lesser amount. Our assessment of repair costs or market value is based on current Australian market data and comparable vehicles.

### MOTOR-3.4 Repair Arrangements
Following a claim, you must obtain our written approval before commencing repairs. We may require you to obtain quotes from preferred repairers or independent assessors. If you proceed with repairs without our approval, we reserve the right to reduce our settlement to reflect the cost we would have paid had we been consulted.

### MOTOR-3.5 Salvage and Recoveries
If your vehicle is a total loss, we become entitled to the salvage and all remaining parts of the vehicle. Any recoveries we make from third parties responsible for the loss will be retained by us and credited against our claim payment. You must cooperate fully with our recovery efforts and provide all necessary documentation and authority.

### MOTOR-3.6 Exclusions to Vehicle Damage Cover
We will not pay for damage caused by or arising from racing or speed trials, use of the vehicle for hire or reward (unless declared), driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or damage to accessories not permanently fitted to the vehicle. We will also not pay for loss or damage that occurs while the vehicle is being driven by a person not permitted under the Permitted Drivers clause of this policy.

## 4. Liability and Additional Benefits Cover

### MOTOR-4.1 Third Party Liability Cover

We will pay on your behalf all sums which you become legally liable to pay as compensation for death, bodily injury or damage to property of third parties, arising out of the use of the insured vehicle. This cover applies up to a maximum of $20,000,000 per occurrence, or such other limit as required by law. We will also pay legal costs and expenses incurred with our prior written consent in defending any claim covered by this section. This liability cover applies whether or not the insured vehicle is the vehicle at fault.

### MOTOR-4.2 Uninsured Motorist Protection

If the insured vehicle is involved in a collision with another motor vehicle whose owner or driver cannot be identified, or whose liability insurer refuses or is unable to meet a valid claim, we will pay for loss or damage to the insured vehicle up to the sum insured, less the applicable excess. This protection is subject to the claim being reported to the police within 24 hours and all reasonable steps being taken to identify the other vehicle. We will not pay under this clause if the insured vehicle was being used for hire or reward purposes.

### MOTOR-4.3 Statutory Liability and Compulsory Third Party

We will maintain statutory liability cover as required under applicable state and territory motor vehicle legislation, including Compulsory Third Party Insurance (CTP) requirements. The minimum limits of liability will comply with the law in the state or territory in which the vehicle is registered. Any amount payable under CTP or statutory liability will not reduce the sum insured for other sections of this policy.

### MOTOR-4.4 Windscreen and Glass Damage

We will pay for accidental breakage or damage to the windscreen, windows, sunroof glass or headlights of the insured vehicle, up to $1,500 per occurrence. This cover applies with a $0 excess if the claim is settled through one of our approved repairers, or with the applicable policy excess if you choose an alternative repairer. We will replace the glass with OEM equivalent or aftermarket equivalent at our discretion.

### MOTOR-4.5 Legal Liability for Passengers

We will indemnify you against legal liability for bodily injury to passengers being carried in the insured vehicle with your permission, up to a maximum of $10,000 per passenger. This cover applies only to claims arising from the operation of the vehicle and does not apply to passengers engaged in the carriage of goods for reward. We will not pay if the passenger was being carried in breach of any law or regulation.

## 5. General Exclusions

### MOTOR-5.1 Wear and Tear
We do not cover loss or damage caused by or arising from wear and tear, depreciation, rust, corrosion, or gradual deterioration of any part of the vehicle. This exclusion applies regardless of whether such deterioration is accelerated by an insured event.

### MOTOR-5.2 Intentional Damage
We do not cover loss or damage that you, or any person claiming under this policy, intentionally cause to the vehicle. This exclusion applies whether or not the damage is caused for the purpose of making a claim.

### MOTOR-5.3 Unlawful Use
We do not cover loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is being used in connection with any unlawful activity, including but not limited to the commission of a crime, or while being driven by a person who is unlawfully using the vehicle. This exclusion does not apply to theft of the vehicle by another party.

### MOTOR-5.4 Mechanical and Electrical Breakdown
We do not cover loss or damage caused by mechanical breakdown, electrical failure, or malfunction of any component of the vehicle, unless such failure is a direct result of an otherwise covered event.

### MOTOR-5.5 Flood
We do not cover loss or damage caused by flood, as defined under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth). This exclusion applies to inundation by water whether or not the water enters the vehicle's interior.

### MOTOR-5.6 Racing and Speed Events
We do not cover loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is being used in any racing, speed trial, pace-making, or competitive speed event, or while being prepared for such an event on a race track or closed circuit.

## 6. Specific Peril and Regional Exclusions

### MOTOR-6.1 Cyclone Damage – Northern Territory and Far North Queensland
We do not cover loss or damage caused by cyclones, including cyclonic winds exceeding 90 kilometres per hour, in the Northern Territory or areas north of the Tropic of Capricorn in Queensland. This exclusion applies whether the cyclone is named or unnamed, and regardless of whether a cyclone warning was issued by the Bureau of Meteorology at the time of loss.

### MOTOR-6.2 Bushfire Damage – Victoria and South Australia
Cover is excluded for any loss, damage, or total loss of the vehicle caused by bushfire, including damage from radiant heat, ember attack, or smoke inhalation of engine components, occurring in Victoria or South Australia during the declared fire season (1 November to 31 March inclusive). We will not cover costs associated with evacuation or relocation of the vehicle to avoid bushfire.

### MOTOR-6.3 Hail Damage – New South Wales Inland Regions
We do not cover damage caused by hail storms to vehicles garaged, parked, or regularly operated west of the Great Dividing Range in New South Wales. This exclusion does not apply if the vehicle is stored in a fully enclosed garage or undercover structure at the time of the hail event.

### MOTOR-6.4 Dust Storm Damage – Western Australia
Loss or damage caused by dust storms, haboobs, or severe dust events in Western Australia is excluded from this policy. This includes damage to paintwork, windscreens, engine intake systems, and mechanical components caused by fine particulate matter during such events.

### MOTOR-6.5 Flash Flooding – Tasmania
We exclude cover for loss or damage to the vehicle caused by flash flooding events in Tasmania, including damage from water ingress, submersion, or debris impact during rapid water rise events in river valleys and low-lying areas. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the vehicle was being actively driven or parked at the time of the event.

## 7. Conditions

### MOTOR-7.1 Duty of Disclosure
You must disclose to us all facts that are material to our decision to accept this insurance and the terms we offer. This includes any previous claims, convictions, traffic offences, and changes to the vehicle or its use. If you fail to disclose a material fact, we may cancel this policy or decline a claim. You must notify us of any changes to the information provided in your application within 14 days.

### MOTOR-7.2 Maintenance and Roadworthiness
You must maintain the vehicle in a safe and roadworthy condition and comply with all applicable registration and safety requirements. We will not pay a claim if the loss or damage has been caused or contributed to by the vehicle being unroadworthy or unregistered. You must ensure all defects are repaired promptly and keep records of servicing and maintenance.

### MOTOR-7.3 Notification of Loss or Damage
You must notify us of any claim, loss, damage, or incident as soon as reasonably practicable and no later than 30 days after the event occurs. You must provide us with full details in writing, including photographs where available, and the contact details of any third parties involved. Failure to notify us promptly may result in us declining your claim.

### MOTOR-7.4 Excess Application
An excess of $400 applies to each and every claim made under this policy. This excess is the amount you must contribute towards the cost of any claim. The excess applies in addition to any other terms, conditions, or limitations of this policy.

### MOTOR-7.5 Authorisation of Repairs
We may require that repairs be carried out by a repairer of our choice or approved by us. You must not proceed with repairs or incur expenses without our written consent, except in cases of emergency where the vehicle poses a safety risk. We will not reimburse repair costs that exceed our written estimate or that are incurred without our authorisation.

### MOTOR-7.6 Cancellation Rights
We may cancel this policy immediately if you fail to pay the premium, make a fraudulent claim, or breach any material condition of this policy. You may cancel this policy at any time by providing us with written notice. Cancellation will take effect from the date specified in your notice, and any refund of premium will be calculated on a pro-rata basis.

## 8. Claims Process

### MOTOR-8.1 Notice of Claim

You must notify us of any claim as soon as practicable and in any case within 30 days of the incident occurring or the loss being discovered. Notice should be provided by telephone, email, or in writing to our claims department. Failure to notify us promptly may prejudice your claim. You must provide us with all relevant details including the date, time, location, and circumstances of the loss or damage.

### MOTOR-8.2 Provision of Information and Documentation

Following notification of a claim, you must provide us with all information and documentation reasonably requested to assess the claim, including the original policy document, proof of ownership, maintenance records, and any police reports or third-party correspondence. You must cooperate fully with our investigation and provide accurate and truthful information at all times. Any misrepresentation or omission of material facts may result in claim denial or reduction.

### MOTOR-8.3 Assessment and Repair

We will assess the claim and determine whether the loss is covered under this policy. For claims involving damage to the vehicle, we may arrange our own repairer or allow you to obtain independent repair quotes for our approval. The repair cost will be assessed on the basis of restoring the vehicle to its condition immediately before the loss, up to the sum insured of $25,000. You must apply the applicable excess of $400 to any claim payment.

### MOTOR-8.4 Claim Settlement

Once we have assessed the claim and confirmed coverage, we will settle the claim by payment to you or directly to a repairer, at our discretion. Settlement will be made within 10 business days of all required documentation being received and the claim being approved. If the cost of repair exceeds the sum insured, we will pay the sum insured less the excess, and the vehicle may be deemed a total loss.

### MOTOR-8.5 Subrogation Rights

We reserve the right to recover amounts paid under this policy from any third party found to be liable for the loss or damage. You must cooperate with us in pursuing any such recovery and must not settle or admit liability with any third party without our written consent. Any amounts recovered will be applied first to our costs, then to the excess, with any remainder returned to you.

## 9. Complaints and Dispute Resolution

### MOTOR-9.1 Internal Complaints Procedure

If you wish to lodge a complaint about this policy or our handling of a claim, please contact our Customer Relations team in writing, by telephone, or via our website. We will acknowledge your complaint within one business day and provide you with a detailed response within ten business days, or if this is not possible, we will advise you of the expected timeframe and keep you informed of progress. If we cannot resolve the matter to your satisfaction, you may refer the dispute to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA).

### MOTOR-9.2 Australian Financial Complaints Authority

AFCA is an independent body that resolves disputes between consumers and financial firms at no cost to you. You may lodge a complaint with AFCA if you are dissatisfied with our response or if we have not responded within the timeframes set out above. AFCA can be contacted at GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001, by telephone on 1800 931 678, or via their website at www.afca.org.au. AFCA's determination is binding on us, subject to your right to pursue other remedies available under law.

### MOTOR-9.3 Dispute Resolution and Governing Law

Any dispute arising from this policy shall be governed by the laws of Australia and the applicable state or territory in which you reside. Nothing in this clause prevents you from pursuing legal remedies available to you under the Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) or other applicable legislation.
