# Travel Insurance Policy Wording

## Declarations

- Policy Limit: $250,000
- Excess: $300

## 1. Definitions

### TRAVEL-1.1 Insured Person
The Insured Person means any person named in the Policy Schedule who is a permanent resident of Australia at the time of purchase and has paid the required premium. Each Insured Person must be under 85 years of age at the time of claim, unless otherwise approved in writing by Us. An Insured Person may include the policyholder's spouse, de facto partner, or dependent children travelling together on the same trip.

### TRAVEL-1.2 Trip
A Trip means a journey undertaken by an Insured Person that commences on the Departure Date shown in the Policy Schedule and concludes on the Return Date, provided the total duration does not exceed 365 consecutive days. The Trip must be undertaken for purposes of leisure, business meetings, or visiting family, and must involve travel outside Australia. A Trip does not include any journey undertaken primarily for the purpose of obtaining medical treatment.

### TRAVEL-1.3 Claim
A Claim means a written request for indemnity submitted by an Insured Person or their legal representative within 90 days of the event giving rise to the loss or damage. The Claim must be accompanied by supporting documentation as reasonably requested by Us, including receipts, invoices, medical reports, or police reports as applicable. We may decline a Claim if the Insured Person fails to provide requested information within 30 days of Our request.

### TRAVEL-1.4 Excess
The Excess means the amount of AUD $300 that the Insured Person must contribute towards each and every Claim, except where otherwise stated in this Policy. The Excess is applied before We pay any benefit under this Policy and is not refundable. Multiple claims arising from the same event or circumstance shall be treated as a single claim for the purposes of applying the Excess.

### TRAVEL-1.5 Maximum Benefit
The Maximum Benefit means the total amount of AUD $250,000 that We will pay under this Policy in respect of any one Insured Person for all claims arising from a single Trip. Once the Maximum Benefit has been paid, all cover under this Policy ceases and no further claims will be entertained for that Trip. The Maximum Benefit applies regardless of the number of claims made or the circumstances giving rise to those claims.

## 2. Significant Features and Benefits

### TRAVEL-2.1 Emergency Medical and Dental Cover

This policy provides cover for emergency medical treatment, hospitalisation, and emergency dental treatment (limited to pain relief) incurred overseas up to the policy limit of $250,000. Cover applies only where treatment is medically necessary and cannot be reasonably delayed until your return to Australia. Emergency medical evacuation and repatriation to Australia are included where medically recommended by a qualified medical practitioner.

### TRAVEL-2.2 Trip Cancellation and Curtailment

You are covered for non-refundable prepaid travel costs if you cancel your trip before departure due to a covered event, or if you curtail your trip and return to Australia early for a valid claim reason. Covered events include serious illness or injury to you or a travelling companion, death of a close relative, and involuntary redundancy. A maximum of $250,000 applies per claim, less the applicable excess of $300.

### TRAVEL-2.3 Baggage and Personal Effects Cover

Loss, damage, or theft of baggage and personal effects during your trip is covered up to the policy limit, including cover for baggage delay of more than 24 hours. Valuables including jewellery, cameras, and electronic devices are subject to a sub-limit of $2,500 per item unless declared separately. The $300 excess applies to each baggage claim.

### TRAVEL-2.4 Overseas Liability and Legal Costs

This policy covers your legal liability for accidental bodily injury to third parties or accidental damage to third-party property occurring during your trip, up to $250,000. Associated legal defence costs and court expenses are included within this limit. Cover does not apply to liability arising from professional activities, motor vehicle operation, or contractual obligations.

## 3. Trip Cancellation and Cover

### TRAVEL-3.1 Cancellation of Trip

If You cancel Your trip before departure due to an Insured Event occurring after this policy commences, We will reimburse You for non-recoverable trip costs up to the policy limit of $250,000, less the excess of $300. An Insured Event includes the death, serious injury, or serious illness of You, a travelling companion, or an immediate family member, or the death of a person with whom You have contracted to stay. Cancellation must occur before Your scheduled departure date, and You must provide documentary evidence supporting the reason for cancellation.

### TRAVEL-3.2 Definition of Trip Costs

Trip costs are defined as prepaid, non-recoverable accommodation, transport, and tour arrangements that You have paid for in advance of Your trip. This includes flights, hotel bookings, package tours, and activity fees that cannot be refunded by the service provider. Trip costs do not include travel insurance premiums, visa fees, or costs that have already been recovered from another source. You must provide receipts and booking confirmations to substantiate all claimed trip costs.

### TRAVEL-3.3 Exclusions to Cancellation Cover

We will not pay for cancellation claims arising from financial hardship, change of mind, or failure to obtain necessary travel documentation. Claims are excluded if the Insured Event was known to You at the time this policy was purchased, or if You were aware of circumstances that would likely result in cancellation. Cancellation due to war, civil unrest, or government travel warnings in effect before the policy commencement date are also excluded. Additionally, claims relating to pre-existing medical conditions are excluded unless You declared them and We accepted the declaration in writing.

### TRAVEL-3.4 Claims Notification and Evidence

You must notify Us of Your intention to claim within 30 days of the cancellation event occurring. You must provide original receipts, booking confirmations, written cancellation confirmation from service providers, and medical certificates (where applicable) to support Your claim. Failure to provide requested documentation within 60 days of notification may result in claim denial. We reserve the right to request additional information to verify the validity of the Insured Event.

### TRAVEL-3.5 Proportionate Recovery

If You recover any refund or credit from the service provider, airline, or accommodation provider after We have paid Your claim, You must repay Us the amount recovered or the claim payment, whichever is lesser. If You receive compensation from another insurance policy or travel protection scheme for the same trip costs, We will only pay the difference between the total costs and amounts already recovered. This clause ensures You do not receive double recovery for the same loss.

### TRAVEL-3.6 Trip Modification and Curtailment

If You modify Your trip by cancelling only part of Your prepaid arrangements after an Insured Event, We will reimburse the non-recoverable costs of the cancelled portion only. If You curtail Your trip early and return home due to an Insured Event, We will reimburse the unused portion of prepaid accommodation and transport, provided You provide evidence of the Insured Event and proof of early departure. The excess of $300 applies to each separate claim for trip modification or curtailment.

## 4. Medical and Emergency Cover

### TRAVEL-4.1 Emergency Medical Treatment Overseas
We will reimburse reasonable and necessary costs of emergency medical treatment received outside Australia, up to the policy limit of $250,000. This includes hospital accommodation, surgical procedures, diagnostic testing, and prescribed medications required as a direct result of sudden illness or injury during your period of cover. Treatment must be provided by a qualified medical practitioner or licensed healthcare facility. Claims are subject to the applicable excess of $300.

### TRAVEL-4.2 Medical Evacuation and Repatriation
We will cover the cost of emergency medical evacuation by air ambulance or other suitable transport to the nearest adequate medical facility, and subsequent repatriation to Australia if medically necessary. These costs are included within the overall policy limit and are only payable where evacuation is deemed medically essential by the treating physician and approved by our claims team. We will arrange and coordinate all evacuation services directly with our international medical assistance network.

### TRAVEL-4.3 Pre-Existing Medical Conditions
Cover for pre-existing medical conditions is excluded unless the condition has been declared to us in writing and specifically endorsed on your policy schedule prior to travel commencement. If a pre-existing condition is disclosed and accepted, any claims arising from that condition remain subject to the policy excess and all other terms and conditions. We reserve the right to request medical evidence or impose additional premiums or restrictions.

### TRAVEL-4.4 Emergency Dental Treatment
We will reimburse emergency dental treatment up to $500 for pain relief or temporary repair of a tooth damaged by accident during your trip. Routine dental work, cosmetic procedures, and treatment for pre-existing dental conditions are not covered. Any claim must be supported by itemised receipts and a statement from the treating dentist confirming the emergency nature of the treatment.

### TRAVEL-4.5 Pharmacy and Prescribed Medications
We will reimburse the reasonable cost of prescription medications and essential pharmacy items required during your trip, provided they are prescribed by a licensed medical practitioner and obtained from a registered pharmacy. Over-the-counter medications, vitamins, and supplements are not covered unless prescribed as emergency treatment for acute illness or injury. All claims must be supported by original receipts and prescription documentation.

## 5. General Exclusions

### TRAVEL-5.1 Wear and Tear
This policy does not cover loss, damage or liability arising from ordinary wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or depreciation of any item or service. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the item was in use at the time the loss occurred.

### TRAVEL-5.2 Unlawful Activities
We will not provide cover for any claim arising directly or indirectly from your participation in unlawful activities, criminal conduct, or violation of any applicable law or regulation in Australia or abroad. This exclusion applies whether or not you were convicted of an offence.

### TRAVEL-5.3 Intentional Damage
This policy excludes cover for loss or damage that you intentionally caused or that resulted from your deliberate or reckless disregard for the safety of persons or property. Claims arising from wilful misconduct are not covered under any section of this policy.

### TRAVEL-5.4 Flood
We do not cover loss, damage, liability or expense arising from or contributed to by flood, being the covering of normally dry land by water that has overflowed from a river, lake, or ocean, or from accumulated rainfall. This exclusion applies to all sections of the policy and is not limited by the amount of water involved or the duration of inundation.

### TRAVEL-5.5 Claims Not Notified Promptly
No claim will be accepted if you fail to notify us of the incident within 30 days of its occurrence, or within a reasonable timeframe if circumstances beyond your control prevented earlier notification. We reserve the right to decline or reduce a claim if we are materially prejudiced by the delay in notification.

### TRAVEL-5.6 Consequential Loss
This policy does not cover consequential loss, indirect loss, loss of profits, loss of enjoyment, loss of opportunity, or any other loss that does not directly result from the insured event. This exclusion applies even if such loss was reasonably foreseeable at the time of the claim.

## 6. Specific Peril and Regional Exclusions

### TRAVEL-6.1 Cyclone Exclusion – Queensland
We do not cover any loss, damage, injury or expense arising from cyclones, tropical storms or related wind events in Queensland, including the period from 1 November to 30 April in any year. This exclusion applies whether or not a cyclone is officially named or declared by the Bureau of Meteorology.

### TRAVEL-6.2 Bushfire Exclusion – New South Wales
We do not cover any loss, damage, injury or expense directly or indirectly caused by bushfires, wildfires, or prescribed burns in New South Wales. This exclusion extends to smoke inhalation, evacuation costs, and property damage resulting from fire activity.

### TRAVEL-6.3 Cyclone and Storm Exclusion – Northern Territory
We do not cover any loss, damage, injury or expense arising from cyclones, severe tropical storms, or associated flooding in the Northern Territory during the official cyclone season (1 November to 30 April). Claims related to storm surge or wind damage are excluded under this clause.

### TRAVEL-6.4 Bushfire Exclusion – South Australia
We do not cover any loss, damage, injury or expense caused by bushfires or wildfires in South Australia, particularly during the declared fire danger season. This includes costs incurred due to evacuation orders, road closures, or smoke-related health events.

### TRAVEL-6.5 Severe Weather Exclusion – Tasmania
We do not cover any loss, damage, injury or expense arising from severe storms, extreme winds exceeding 90 kilometres per hour, or associated lightning strikes in Tasmania. This exclusion does not apply to standard rainfall or moderate weather conditions.

## 7. Conditions

### TRAVEL-7.1 Duty of Disclosure

You must disclose all material facts that are relevant to our assessment of this insurance at the time of application. A material fact is any information that would influence our decision to accept your application or the terms and conditions we offer. Failure to disclose material facts may result in cancellation of this policy or a claim being denied. You have a continuing obligation to notify us of any changes to the information provided in your application.

### TRAVEL-7.2 Excess Application

An excess of $300 applies to each and every claim made under this policy, except where otherwise stated in the policy schedule. The excess must be paid by you before we settle any claim. Where a claim is denied in full, no excess is payable. Multiple claims arising from the same event or occurrence are treated as a single claim for the purposes of excess calculation.

### TRAVEL-7.3 Claims Notification

You must notify us of any claim as soon as practicable and in any event within 90 days of the event giving rise to the claim. Written notice must include your policy number, details of the incident, and supporting documentation as requested. Failure to notify us within this timeframe may result in denial of your claim if we are materially prejudiced by the delay. We may extend this period at our discretion where reasonable circumstances exist.

### TRAVEL-7.4 Provision of Evidence

You must provide all documentation, receipts, invoices, medical reports, and other evidence reasonably required to substantiate your claim. We may request independent verification of any claim, including medical examination or police reports, at our cost. You must cooperate fully with any investigation we conduct and provide information in a timely manner. Failure to provide requested evidence within 30 days may result in claim denial.

### TRAVEL-7.5 Policy Limit and Aggregate Cover

The maximum we will pay under this policy for any single claim is $250,000. The total amount we will pay across all claims during the period of insurance cannot exceed the policy limit stated in your schedule. Once the policy limit has been exhausted, no further claims will be accepted under this policy. Claims are settled on an indemnity basis and you cannot recover more than your actual loss.

### TRAVEL-7.6 Cancellation and Variation

You may cancel this policy at any time by providing written notice to us. If you cancel before the commencement date, we will refund the full premium. If you cancel after commencement, we will refund the pro-rata premium less any claims paid and a $50 administration fee. We may cancel this policy by providing 14 days' written notice if you breach a material condition or if you fail to pay the premium.

## 8. Claims Process

### TRAVEL-8.1 Notification of Claim
You must notify us of a claim as soon as practicable and in any case within 30 days of the event giving rise to the claim, or within 30 days of discovering the loss or damage. Notification may be made by telephone, email, or in writing to our claims department using the contact details provided in your policy documents. Failure to notify us within this timeframe may result in your claim being rejected or reduced if we are materially prejudiced by the delay.

### TRAVEL-8.2 Supporting Documentation
You must provide us with all documentation reasonably required to assess your claim, including receipts, invoices, proof of payment, medical reports, police reports (where applicable), airline statements, and any other evidence supporting your claim. Original documents should be provided where possible; however, certified copies or digital scans are acceptable. We may request additional information at any time during the claims assessment process, and you must provide such information within 14 days of our request.

### TRAVEL-8.3 Claims Assessment and Payment
We will assess your claim in accordance with the terms and conditions of this policy and will advise you of our decision within 30 days of receiving all required documentation. If your claim is approved, payment will be made by electronic transfer to your nominated bank account within 10 business days of approval. The maximum amount we will pay for any single claim is the policy limit of AUD $250,000, less the applicable excess of AUD $300 and any amounts already paid under this policy during the period of insurance.

### TRAVEL-8.4 Excess Application
An excess of AUD $300 applies to each and every claim made under this policy, regardless of the number of claims or the cause of loss. The excess is the amount you must contribute toward each claim, and this will be deducted from any benefit payment we make. No excess applies to claims for emergency medical evacuation or claims arising from a claim event occurring prior to the commencement date of this policy.

### TRAVEL-8.5 Dispute Resolution
If you are dissatisfied with our claims decision, you may lodge a formal dispute in writing within 30 days of receiving our decision. We will review your dispute and provide a response within 21 days. If the dispute remains unresolved, you may refer the matter to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) in accordance with the dispute resolution procedures outlined in your policy documents.

## 9. Complaints and Dispute Resolution

### TRAVEL-9.1 Internal Complaints Process

If you are dissatisfied with our service or the handling of your claim, you may lodge a complaint in writing to our Customer Relations Team at the address shown in your policy documents. We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within one (1) business day and provide you with a detailed response within ten (10) business days, or if this is not possible, we will explain the reasons for the delay and provide a timeframe for resolution. Where we uphold your complaint, we will take appropriate remedial action at no cost to you.

### TRAVEL-9.2 External Dispute Resolution

If you remain dissatisfied after our internal response, or if we fail to respond within the timeframes specified, you may refer your complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) at no cost to you. AFCA is an independent body that reviews complaints about financial services providers and can award compensation up to the limits prescribed under the AFCA Rules. You may contact AFCA by telephone, email, or in writing using the details available on the AFCA website.

### TRAVEL-9.3 Dispute Regarding Claim Denial

Where we deny or reduce a claim under this policy, you have the right to request a detailed written explanation of our decision within thirty (30) days of notification. If you dispute our decision and cannot reach agreement through the internal complaints process, either party may elect to pursue the matter through AFCA or, by mutual written consent, through independent expert determination or mediation, with costs to be determined by the dispute resolution process selected.
